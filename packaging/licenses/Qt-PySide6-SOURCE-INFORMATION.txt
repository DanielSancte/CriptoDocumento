ProtectorPDF - Qt / PySide6 source and relinking information
============================================================

This Windows distribution uses the open-source LGPL-3.0-only option offered
by PySide6, PySide6-Essentials and shiboken6 version 6.12.0.  It redistributes
the Qt 6.12.0 libraries used by the application as separate, unmodified DLLs.

information applies to the ProtectorPDF distribution built from that lock.

Replacement and debugging
-------------------------

Qt and PySide are dynamically linked.  A recipient may replace the shipped
Qt/PySide DLLs with compatible modified builds that preserve the expected ABI.
ProtectorPDF imposes no additional contractual restriction on reverse
engineering performed to debug modifications to those LGPL-covered libraries.
The application's own files do not contain a technical measure intended to
prevent such replacement.

Qt incorporates additional third-party code.  Its exact inventory for the Qt
modules present in ProtectorPDF is recorded in THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md.  The
authoritative notices and source files for those components are in the Qt
6.12.0 source archives above.
